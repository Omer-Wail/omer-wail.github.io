NLP research · 2026

When is an idiom literal?

Problem
Can a model use the surrounding English text to tell whether a phrase carries its literal or idiomatic meaning?
Decision
I fine-tuned BERT on the MAGPIE dataset. Literal and idiomatic examples appeared at different rates, so I used a weighted loss to account for the imbalance.
Result
The model reached 83% accuracy and a weighted F1 of 0.83 on 4,840 held-out examples. I then examined the misclassifications to understand where the model fell short.

Assistive technology · natHacks 2026

Clarté

Problem
How can someone with dysarthria and a motor disability select an option in a speech assist app without a mouse or touchscreen?
Decision
I simplified the interface to a 2×4 grid. The large targets require less precision from webcam eye tracking.
Result
I built a prototype that lets users select on-screen options with their eyes.
